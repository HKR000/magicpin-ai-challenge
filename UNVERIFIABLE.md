## UV-01: Closed-Door Frontier LLM Judge Provider Variance

* **Requirement**:
  - `challenge-testing-brief.md` §1 & `EVALUATION_SPEC.md` §1: The official competition evaluation runs an automated judge powered by an undisclosed commercial frontier LLM (e.g., GPT-4o or Claude 3.5 Sonnet) playing the role of merchants and scoring outputs.
* **Why it Cannot be Verified**:
  - In the local workspace environment, no active third-party LLM API keys (`OPENAI_API_KEY`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `GEMINI_API_KEY`) are configured.
  - The local evaluation relies on `judge_simulator.py` operating under `LLM_PROVIDER="canonical"` (the deterministic rule-based rubric simulator that implements the exact 5-dimension rubric from `EVALUATION_SPEC.md` §2).
  - While the canonical simulator yields an objective score of 47/50 (94%), the qualitative scoring variance and conversational nuances of an external frontier LLM evaluating free-form Hinglish text cannot be verified offline.
* **Missing Evidence**:
  - Closed-door live evaluation logs and transcripts generated by magicpin's remote judge harness.
* **What Must be Executed/Inspected**:
  - Connect `judge_simulator.py` to an active frontier API key (`export LLM_PROVIDER=openai; export OPENAI_API_KEY=...`) to observe non-deterministic evaluator variance.
