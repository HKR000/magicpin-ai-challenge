## 1. Overview of Evaluation Architecture

The magicpin AI evaluation harness uses an LLM-powered judge that acts in three simultaneous capacities:
1. **Context Injector**: Feeds initial and real-time streaming context to the bot via `/v1/context`.
2. **Simulated Actor**: Simulates realistic, context-aware WhatsApp behavior of Indian merchants and their retail customers.
3. **Strict Evaluator**: Scores every proactive outbound message (`/v1/tick`) across five 0–10 dimensions, evaluates multi-turn conversational competence (`/v1/reply`), applies operational penalties, and calculates cumulative performance.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               magicpin Judge Simulator                 │
├─────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────┤
│ Context Controller  │ Pushes initial + adaptive delta  │
│ Actor Simulator     │ Emulates merchant/customer turns │
│ Evaluator & Scorer  │ 5-Dimension Rubric + Penalties   │
└──────────┬──────────┴──────────────────▲───────────────┘
           │ (HTTP calls)                │ (HTTP responses)
           ▼                             │
┌────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────┐
│               Candidate Bot System                     │
│  (FastAPI Server, Context Store, Decision Engine,       │
│   WhatsApp Composer, Multi-Turn Reply Handler)         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Core Scoring Rubric (5 Dimensions × 10 Points = 50 Max)

Every proactive action generated by the bot is scored on a 0–10 scale across five orthogonal dimensions. Total message score is:
$$\text{Score}_{\text{message}} = \max\left(0, \sum_{d=1}^{5} \text{Dim}_d - \sum \text{Penalties}\right)$$

### 2.1 Dimension 1: Specificity (0–10)
- **Objective**: Anchoring on concrete, verifiable facts from the provided context.
- **Scoring Standard**:
  - **10 (Flawless)**: Message contains exact figures (e.g., "₹299", "2,100-patient trial", "38% better", "6,777 missed searches", exact appointment slots "Wed 5 Nov, 6pm"), exact source citations ("JIDA Oct 2026 p.14"), and specific locality anchors.
  - **7–9 (Strong)**: Cites accurate numbers and specific offers, but misses minor granular anchors (e.g., omits specific study page or exact city locality).
  - **4–6 (Mediocre)**: Generic assertions with weak facts ("many patients", "improve your views", "discount available").
  - **0–3 (Failure)**: Pure generic boilerplate ("Boost your business!", "10% off everything"), or uses hallucinated numbers not present in the payload.

### 2.2 Dimension 2: Category Fit (0–10)
- **Objective**: Voice, tone, register, and offer structures authentic to the vertical.
- **Scoring Standard**:
  - **10 (Flawless)**: Tone perfectly matches the domain voice profile.
    - *Dentists*: Peer-clinical, respectful, collegial, technical jargon permitted ("caries", "fluoride varnish"), zero legal/medical overclaims.
    - *Salons*: Inviting, visual, service+price ("Haircut @ ₹99"), appropriate emojis, respectful styling advice.
    - *Restaurants*: High-velocity, appetite-stimulating, match-day/festival timely, delivery/dine-in combo clarity.
    - *Gyms*: Motivating, habit-focused, seasonal dip recovery, membership/pass pricing clarity.
    - *Pharmacies*: Compliance-accurate, chronic refill diligence, zero diagnosis claims.
  - **7–9 (Strong)**: Category-appropriate tone with slight stylistic imperfections.
  - **4–6 (Mediocre)**: Tone mismatch (e.g., treating a dental specialist like a discount grocery store).
  - **0–3 (Failure)**: Violates explicit vertical taboos (e.g., using "100% guaranteed cure" for dentists/pharmacies).

### 2.3 Dimension 3: Merchant Fit (0–10)
- **Objective**: Personalization reflecting the specific merchant's operational and conversational state.
- **Scoring Standard**:
  - **10 (Flawless)**: Seamlessly incorporates merchant-specific state:
    - Greets by owner first name (`owner_first_name`) or formal title (`Dr. {first_name}`).
    - References their verified catalog offers, actual 7d/30d performance trends, or specific customer cohort ("your 124 high-risk adult patients").
    - Strictly honors language preference (`languages`: Hindi-English code-mix, English, etc.).
    - Demonstrates awareness of prior conversation history (no repeating what was discussed 2 days ago).
  - **7–9 (Strong)**: Good merchant personalization with minor generic phrasing.
  - **4–6 (Mediocre)**: Uses merchant name but ignores performance signals, language preference, or offer status.
  - **0–3 (Failure)**: Misattributes merchant name, recommends expired offers as active, or promotes irrelevant services.

### 2.4 Dimension 4: Decision Quality & Trigger Relevance (0–10)
- **Objective**: Picking the right signal and clearly explaining *why now*.
- **Scoring Standard**:
  - **10 (Flawless)**: The trigger is the explicit driving force of the outreach.
    - External trigger: Explains immediately why the regulation change, research release, or festival matters *today*.
    - Internal trigger: Directs immediate attention to the severe performance drop, upcoming expiration, or customer recall deadline.
    - Selects the single highest-priority trigger among candidates; respects suppression rules.
  - **7–9 (Strong)**: Connects trigger to action effectively but could sharpen the "why now" link.
  - **4–6 (Mediocre)**: Mentions trigger incidentally while delivering a generic sales pitch.
  - **0–3 (Failure)**: Trigger is disconnected from message body or completely ignored.

### 2.5 Dimension 5: Engagement Compulsion (0–10)
- **Objective**: Creating a compelling psychological reason to reply with near-zero friction.
- **Scoring Standard**:
  - **10 (Flawless)**: Leverages at least one proven psychological compulsion lever:
    - *Curiosity*: "Want to see who?" / "Worth a look (2-min read)?"
    - *Effort Externalization*: "I've already drafted 3 posts — just reply YES to review."
    - *Loss Aversion*: "People are searching for aligners in Lajpat Nagar but not finding you."
    - *Single Binary CTA*: Low-cognitive load commitment (YES/STOP or choice between 2 concrete slots).
    - *Reciprocity*: "I noticed your calls dipped 50% this week — pulled the search trends for you."
  - **7–9 (Strong)**: Clear single CTA with moderate reply incentive.
  - **4–6 (Mediocre)**: Open-ended, high-effort request ("Can you review your entire menu and let me know?").
  - **0–3 (Failure)**: Multiple conflicting CTAs, buried CTA, or zero reason to respond.

## 3. Operational Deductions & Penalties

The judge enforces strict automated penalties for non-compliant or degraded bot behavior:

| Violation | Severity / Penalty | Triggering Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Malformed JSON** | **-2 points** per event | Any non-JSON or schema-invalid response from `/v1/tick` or `/v1/reply`. |
| **Empty Send Body** | **-2 points** per event | Bot returns `{"action": "send"}` with an empty or whitespace body. |
| **Verbatim Repetition** | **-2 points** per event | Bot repeats the same text verbatim within the same conversation session. |
| **Response Timeout** | **-1 point** per occurrence | Any endpoint call taking longer than 30.0 seconds. |
| **Healthz Probe Failure** | **Disqualification** (-10 operational) | 3 consecutive failed health check probes (`/v1/healthz`). |
| **Hallucination** | **Zero score on Specificity** (0/10) | Fabricating prices, studies, patient names, dates, or competitor metrics not present in context. |

## 4. Test Lifecycle & Judge Execution Phases

The judge runs a structured 5-phase evaluation mimicking real-world deployment:

```
[Phase 1: Warmup] ──► [Phase 2: Test Window] ──► [Phase 3: Adaptive Context]
                                                      │
[Phase 5: Final Report] ◄── [Phase 4: Replay Test (Top 10)] ◄┘
```

### Phase 1: Warmup (T-15 min)
1. Judge tests `GET /v1/healthz` and `GET /v1/metadata`.
2. Judge pushes the full base dataset via `POST /v1/context`:
   - 5 categories (`dentists`, `salons`, `restaurants`, `gyms`, `pharmacies`)
   - 50 merchants (10 per category)
   - 200 customers
3. Re-checks `/v1/healthz`: `contexts_loaded` must accurately register all 255 base records.

### Phase 2: Simulated Test Window (60 Simulated Minutes)
- Simulated clock advances in 5-minute ticks.
- At each tick, judge POSTs `available_triggers` to `POST /v1/tick`.
- Candidate bot returns zero or more proactive `actions[]`.
- For each generated action, the judge invokes an LLM playing the recipient (merchant or customer) and posts their reply to `POST /v1/reply`.
- Multi-turn conversation flows up to 5 turns or until the bot emits `action: "end"` or `action: "wait"`.

### Phase 3: Adaptive Context Injection (Surprise Delta Test)
- Judge injects unseen data mid-test:
  - 5 brand-new research/compliance items per category pushed as higher versions.
  - Performance delta shifts for 10 merchants (e.g., sudden drop in calls or spike in views).
  - 15 unexpected trigger events.
  - Surprise customer contexts with urgent `recall_due` triggers.
- **Scoring Impact**: Up to **+5 adaptation bonus** per dimension for bots that immediately ground subsequent decisions on the updated context version.

### Phase 4: Replay Test (Top 10 Finalists)
The 10 highest-ranked bots from Phase 2/3 undergo three stress-test conversational replays (5 turns each, up to +30 points total):
1. **Auto-Reply Hell**:
   - Judge plays a merchant account sending an identical canned WhatsApp business message ("Thank you for reaching out...") 4 turns in a row.
   - *Success Criterion*: Bot must detect repetition and emit `action: "end"` (or `action: "wait"`) by turn 2 or 3.
2. **Intent Transition**:
   - Judge plays an engaged merchant who commits ("Ok let's do it. What's next?").
   - *Success Criterion*: Bot must switch immediately to action execution (`action: "send"` with actionable onboarding/update steps). It must NOT ask further qualification questions.
3. **Hostile / Off-Topic**:
   - Merchant sends an abusive/opt-out message ("Stop messaging me. This is spam.") followed by an off-topic query.
   - *Success Criterion*: Bot apologizes politely, stays strictly on mission, and gracefully terminates (`action: "end"`).

### Phase 5: Scoring Aggregation & Tier Classification
- Total points accumulated determine final placement:
  - **>= 80% (40–50 / 50)**: **EXCELLENT** (Competition-winning tier, production ready).
  - **60%–79% (30–39 / 50)**: **GOOD** (Solid engineering, minor stylistic or conversational flaws).
  - **40%–59% (20–29 / 50)**: **NEEDS IMPROVEMENT** (Functional bot with frequent generic copy or missed signals).
  - **< 40% (0–19 / 50)**: **BELOW EXPECTATIONS** (Failure, high hallucinations, or brittle endpoint behavior).

## 6. Documented Unknowns & Gaps

In accordance with Level 1 exit criteria, the following items are underspecified or ambiguous in the source challenge materials:

1. **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API Template Registry**:
   - The brief requires `template_name` and `template_params` on the first proactive message to honor WhatsApp's 24-hour window, but there is no external pre-registered Meta template catalog. The spec notes: *"use any sensible template structure with `{{1}}/{{2}}/...` parameters; we won't actually call Meta"*. The exact template schema naming convention is therefore flexible so long as parameter arrays match the message placeholders.
2. **Context Cleanup / Teardown Endpoint**:
   - Section 11 of `challenge-testing-brief.md` mentions that magicpin will issue an *optional* `POST /v1/teardown` at the conclusion of testing. The exact request payload for `/v1/teardown` is not formally defined in the contract; our system should handle both empty bodies and `{"wipe": true}` gracefully.
3. **Simulated Clock Skew in `/v1/tick`**:
   - `TickBody.now` provides an ISO-8601 string of simulated time. The brief does not state whether trigger expiration checks should strictly use `TickBody.now` or system wall-clock time. *Resolution*: Systems must evaluate `expires_at` against `TickBody.now` whenever supplied to ensure deterministic simulation replay.
4. **LLM Judge Provider for Final Evaluation**:
   - `judge_simulator.py` supports multiple LLM backends (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Ollama, DeepSeek). The official magicpin judging harness runs an undisclosed frontier model behind closed doors. The bot must be model-agnostic and strictly self-contained.
